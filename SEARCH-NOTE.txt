v3.5 search behavior
- Searches currently loaded live aircraft first.
- Global lookup distinguishes registration, ICAO 24-bit address, and broadcast ADS-B callsign.
- An airline flight number is not assumed to equal the broadcast callsign.
- Search is live-only: aircraft no longer transmitting cannot be found through the live ADS-B endpoints.
- Position-aware route lookup remains separate and only trusted when the route response is plausible.
